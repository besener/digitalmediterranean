Export Your StoryMap

Step 1

Open your StoryMap, select the ellipsis, and choose Print preview.


Step 2

Scroll through your print preview and ensure everything looks good, then select Print.


Step 3

Save your document as a PDF.

💡 Tip: Proper naming conventions are key to keeping your digital portfolio organized. For example, using `ARTHIST329_LastnameFirstname_Title.docx` for your file name is a good choice.
