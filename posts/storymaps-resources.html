Need to find images or digital collections?

Check out the short list of digital collections below, where you can download high-resolution images and interact with medieval maps for free:

- Bodleian Library Digital Collections
- Library of Congress
- David Rumsey's Historical Map Collection at Stanford University
- Hereford Mappa Mundi
- Fra Mauro's World Map
- Mapping the remnants of Byzantine Constantinople
- Virtual Mappa: Digital Editions of Medieval Maps of the World 
- Matthew Paris Annotated Map
- Mapping Mandeville Project
- The Religious Geography of Venice
