Need to find images or digital collections?

A list of digital collections you can download images from (always make sure to cite them!):

- Bodleian Library Digital Collections.
- Library of Congress.
- David Rumsey's Historical Map Collection at Stanford University.
- Hereford Mappa Mundi.
- Fra Mauro's World Map.

Want to learn more about GIS?
- Check out the Library's GIS research guide
- The Environmental Studies program at NU offers introductory GIS courses on GIS
- The Anthropology Department at NU hosts an open Spatial and Cartographic Analysis of Landscape and Environments Lab
